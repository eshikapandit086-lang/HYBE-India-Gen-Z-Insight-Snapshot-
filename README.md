HYBE India — Gen Z Insight Snapshot

Understanding India's K-pop audience through research and digital culture.

The HYBE India Gen Z Insight Snapshot is an interactive, editorial-style research report exploring Indian K-pop audience behaviour, engagement gaps and community-led growth strategies.

It is part of the broader K-Wave Intelligence Project.

Why This Project?

India has an active and growing K-pop audience. Understanding this audience requires looking beyond popularity and exploring how fans engage, participate and build communities around the music and artists they follow.

This project brings those questions into a structured research experience.

What It Explores

- Indian Gen Z and K-pop audience behaviour
- Audience engagement patterns
- Potential engagement gaps
- Fan communities and their role in growth
- Community-led strategies for audience engagement

Format

The project is presented as an interactive, editorial-style research snapshot rather than a traditional research document.

Intended Audience

The report is framed around insights relevant to HYBE India's understanding of the Indian Gen Z audience.

Project Context

Part of the K-Wave Intelligence Project.

Status

Research and interactive presentation project.
